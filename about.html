Who We Are

Your Trusted Travel Partner in Bangladesh

Naria Travels & Tours is a leading travel agency based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Established in 2019, we have been providing top-notch travel services to travelers around the world. Whether you are planning a family vacation, a business trip, or a religious pilgrimage, Naria Travels & Tours has got you covered.

With our team of experienced and dedicated travel professionals, we offer a comprehensive range of travel services including air ticket booking, hotel reservations, tour packages (domestic and international), Umrah and Hajj package booking, and visa processing services.

Our mission is to make travel easy, enjoyable, and perfect for every client. We provide well-trained guides with good communication skills at budgeted rates to help you explore every corner of the world.

Our Mission

To make travel accessible, enjoyable, and hassle-free for everyone. We aim to provide high-quality, personalized travel services that exceed our clients' expectations while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and integrity.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the most trusted and innovative travel agency in Bangladesh, inspiring people to explore the world and create unforgettable memories. We envision making international and domestic travel easy and affordable for every Bangladeshi.

Our Values

Integrity, customer satisfaction, safety, and respect for all cultures and environments. We believe in small group travel that keeps us under the radar, traveling the local way, eating and sleeping like locals.
